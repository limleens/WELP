# WELP — Your Voice-First Home Workout Helper
**Hiya Voice AI Challenge · Real-Time Assistance**

**What I built.** WELP is a hands-free HIIT companion for people exercising at home. It creates an equipment-free circuit from the user's goal, experience, preferred intensity and session length, then guides them through timed movement and recovery intervals. Users can speak naturally to ask what's next, check remaining time, pause, skip an exercise, extend a break or say they're struggling. WELP responds with speech and updates the current workout without requiring a keyboard.

**Who it helps and why voice matters.** People doing home workouts often have their hands occupied, stand away from their screens and need guidance while moving. A voice-first interface makes exercise transitions and adjustments possible in the moment. Unlike a static timer video, WELP adapts both the current session and a future plan to the user's choices and reported difficulty.

**The prototype.** A lightweight browser app combines on-device MediaPipe Pose Landmarker inference for squat/jumping-jack rep counts, a timed HIIT session state machine for work/rest intervals, browser speech recognition and synthesis for coaching, and local persistence for preferences and workout history. Planks are timed but not form-checked. The coach gives context-aware responses, announces transitions and checks in when it observes an extended period without reps while tracking is active. Responding “yes” to a break prompt inserts a rest interval; “make it easier” shortens remaining work intervals and extends recovery. The user can manually correct rep counts.

**Engineering choices.** WELP reuses a pretrained pose model rather than training exercise recognition from scratch. Exercise transitions, progress and adaptations are deterministic and unit-testable; spoken requests are mapped through a bounded, context-sensitive intent parser rather than relying on an unrestricted LLM to generate fitness prescriptions. The app runs locally without an API key or backend; workout data stays in browser storage. Browser speech recognition may use a vendor service, and pose tracking works best with a full-body camera view.

**What I'd improve next.** Benchmark rep-counting errors across different devices, exercise speeds and camera angles; improve speech recognition in noisy workout settings; add richer exercise demonstrations and user-controlled substitutions; introduce optional LLM-based intent mapping behind validated tool actions; and evaluate longer-term personalization with real user feedback. Energy expenditure, where shown, is explicitly a rough optional estimate, not a measurement or goal.

*Prototype only: not medical advice, validated form correction or a substitute for professional exercise guidance. Before submitting, verify claims against a live demo and personalize this write-up with your own test results.*
