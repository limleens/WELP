THE IDEA BEHIND PACEPAL

Less tapping.
More moving.

Home workouts shouldn't require a second pair of hands. PacePal listens to your spoken instructions, observes simple body movements, and suggests future targets based on your own history.

01 / Voice first

Start, pause, ask about progress, and adjust difficulty while your hands are busy.

02 / Real-time vision

Pose landmarks power a transparent squat and jumping-jack rep counter. No custom model training required.

03 / Adaptive goals

Completion and perceived exertion feed a simple, inspectable recommendation rule.

04 / Privacy-minded

Pose analysis runs locally. History stays in your browser. Browser speech recognition may use a cloud service.

Demo and learning prototype only. Does not provide medical advice, injury prevention, or professional exercise assessment.
